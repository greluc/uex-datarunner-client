## 11. How to sign

You sign this Agreement once. The signature covers all of Your present
and future Contributions to the Project unless and until You explicitly
withdraw under [§ 12 — Withdrawal](#12-withdrawal).

### Primary signing path (signature PR)

1. Open a pull request titled `cla: sign — <your-github-handle>`
   against [`docs/cla-signatures.md`](docs/cla-signatures.md), adding a
   new row with:
   - Your full legal name.
   - Your GitHub handle.
   - The e-mail address You use for Git commits. Your GitHub noreply
     address is accepted if You commit with it.
   - The date in ISO-8601 format (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
   - The CLA version You are signing (currently `1.0`).
2. Include the following sign-off in the pull-request description,
   **verbatim**:

   > I have read the UEX Datarunner Client Individual Contributor
   > Licence Agreement, version 1.0, and I hereby accept and agree to
   > its terms for all of my present and future Contributions to the
   > Project.

3. Wait for a Project Maintainer to merge the signature PR before
   opening Your first Contribution PR. The maintainer will verify that
   the e-mail address and name on the signature PR match the commits You
   intend to submit.

[`docs/cla-signatures.md`](docs/cla-signatures.md) is public: the name
and e-mail address You enter there are published with the repository
and remain in its history.

### Entity (corporate) contributors

If You make Contributions on behalf of Your employer or another legal
entity, the entity must execute a separate **Entity CLA**. Contact
[lucas.greuloch@gmail.com](mailto:lucas.greuloch@gmail.com) to obtain the
Entity CLA template. Until the Entity CLA is countersigned, Your
employer-funded Contributions cannot be merged.

### What a maintainer checks before merging Your first PR

- Your GitHub handle appears in [`docs/cla-signatures.md`](docs/cla-signatures.md).
- The author e-mail address on every commit in the PR matches the e-mail address You signed with.
- Every commit carries a `Signed-off-by:` trailer that matches its
  author, as the Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off rule in
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) requires.
