# ADR-0000: Open points register

> **Doc type:** Living reference — current. Last reviewed: 2026-10-08.

This is the single register of open owner decisions and open verifications ([`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), "Requirements, decisions and open points"). No other document keeps its own list; documents link here by ID.

- **IDs** (`O-<n>`) are stable and never reused. A new point takes the next free number.
- **Status:**
  - `open` – not decided or not verified yet;
  - `open – default applied` – the plan already uses the stated default, and @greluc may change it;
  - `decided YYYY-MM-DD (@greluc)` – decided by @greluc;
  - `decided YYYY-MM-DD (default)` – a recommended default adopted in the governance decisions of 2026-10-08; @greluc may veto it.
- Decided points stay in the register with their outcome and where it is recorded. A decided point is reopened only by @greluc.
- Assumptions (`A…`) stay in [01](../plan/01-requirements.md) and the UEX open points in [06](../plan/06-uex-api.md); entries here link them.
